Our Story

Building trust and community in Sri Lankan Real Estate.

Trust & Transparency

Founded in 2016, LankaLand Pvt Ltd has grown from a small consultancy to one of Sri Lanka's leading real estate and land sales agencies. With over 1,000 satisfied customers and hundreds of clear-title land transfers, we pride ourselves on making land purchasing as stress-free as possible.

The Sri Lankan property market is beautiful but complex. Our mission is to provide 100% verified, litigation-free land so that your investment remains a secure and profitable asset for future generations.

Our Process

1. Comprehensive Verification: We conduct a thorough 30-year title search for every plot we list.
2. Infrastructure Integration: Most properties come with water, electricity, and paved road access.
3. Legal Support: From writing deeds to final registration, our in-house legal team manages it all.
